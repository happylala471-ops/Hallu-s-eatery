HERITAGE & TRADITION

The Heart of Hallu's Family Hospitality

Founded on ancestral Ethiopian culinary principles, Habesha Eatery was born from the desire to present the soul of Addis Ababa to discerning food lovers.

Every spice blend — from our deeply complex 15-spice berbere to our fiery mitmita — is sun-dried, roasted, and blended in small batches following recipes guarded across four generations.

We bake our sourdough injera fresh three times daily from 100% pure organic brown and white highland teff, yielding that irreplaceable spongy honeycomb texture and tangy aroma essential for absorbing rich sauces.
